# Government Financial Inclusion Schemes (India)

Source basis: Official scheme portals (pmjdy.gov.in, jansuraksha.gov.in, npscra.nsdl.co.in, India Post / RBI). Premiums and limits can change — users should verify on the official portal or at their bank.

## Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)
- Zero-balance basic savings bank account.
- Free RuPay debit card with accident insurance cover.
- Overdraft facility for eligible account holders.
- Open at any bank branch or Business Correspondent (Bank Mitra) with Aadhaar or other valid ID.

## Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)
- Life insurance cover of ₹2 lakh.
- Age 18–50 years, with a bank or post office account.
- Low yearly premium (about ₹436), auto-debited from the account.

## Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)
- Accidental death/disability cover up to ₹2 lakh.
- Age 18–70 years.
- Very low yearly premium (about ₹20), auto-debited.

## Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
- Guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 after age 60, depending on contribution.
- For people aged 18–40 (mainly unorganised sector workers).

## Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
- Savings scheme for a girl child below 10 years.
- Government-set interest rate, tax benefits; open at a post office or bank.

## Public Provident Fund (PPF)
- 15-year government-backed savings, ₹500 to ₹1.5 lakh per year.
- Tax benefits; safe, long-term.

## PM SVANidhi
- Small working-capital loans for street vendors, with interest subsidy and digital-payment cashback.

## PM Mudra Yojana
- Collateral-free business loans for small/micro enterprises through banks and NBFCs (Shishu, Kishore, Tarun categories).

## Where to get help
- Your bank branch or Bank Mitra (Business Correspondent).
- Common Service Centres (CSC) in villages.
- RBI complaint portal: cms.rbi.org.in.
- Financial literacy centres run by banks.
