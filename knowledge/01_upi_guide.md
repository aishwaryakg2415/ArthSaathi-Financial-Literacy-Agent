# UPI (Unified Payments Interface) – Beginner Guide

Source basis: NPCI (npci.org.in) UPI FAQs, RBI public awareness material ("RBI Kehta Hai").

## What is UPI?
UPI is a real-time payment system run by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India). It lets you send and receive money instantly from your bank account using a mobile app such as BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or your bank's own app. It works 24x7, including holidays.

## What you need
- A bank account linked to your mobile number.
- A smartphone with a UPI app (or a basic phone using UPI 123PAY).
- A debit card (or Aadhaar-based OTP with some banks) to set your UPI PIN the first time.

## How to set up UPI (step by step)
1. Install a UPI app from the official Play Store / App Store.
2. Verify your mobile number (the app sends an SMS from your phone).
3. Select your bank. The app finds the account linked to your number.
4. Set a UPI PIN (4 or 6 digits) using the last 6 digits of your debit card and its expiry date, then the OTP from your bank.
5. Your UPI ID (like name@bank) is created.

## How to send money via UPI
1. Open the UPI app and tap "Send" / "Pay".
2. Choose one: scan the receiver's QR code, enter their UPI ID, or enter their mobile number.
3. Check the receiver's name shown on screen carefully.
4. Enter the amount and an optional note.
5. Enter your UPI PIN to approve. You get a confirmation and a transaction ID (UTR).

## How to receive money
Share your UPI ID or QR code. **You never need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.** If anyone asks you to "enter PIN to receive", it is a scam.

## Collect requests
A "collect request" is someone asking you to pay. Approving it with your PIN sends money OUT of your account. Decline requests you do not recognise.

## Limits (general)
- Most banks allow up to ₹1 lakh per UPI transaction for normal person-to-person payments; certain categories (e.g. tax, hospitals, education, IPO) can have higher limits. Banks may set lower daily limits. Check your bank's app for your exact limit.
- New users may have a lower limit (around ₹5,000) in the first 24 hours.
- UPI Lite is for small, fast payments without entering the PIN each time; it has a small wallet balance limit.

## UPI on a basic (feature) phone – UPI 123PAY
Call an IVR number, use a missed call, or an app on the feature phone. Useful for people without smartphones or internet.

## If a payment fails or money is debited but not received
- Usually the amount is auto-reversed within a few working days.
- Raise a complaint inside the UPI app ("Help" / "Raise dispute") using the transaction ID.
- If unresolved, complain to your bank, then to the RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in.

## Golden safety rules
- Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or card CVV with anyone, including "bank officials".
- PIN is needed only to PAY, never to RECEIVE.
- Do not scan QR codes sent by strangers claiming you will receive money.
- Download apps only from official stores. Never install screen-sharing apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) on a stranger's request.
